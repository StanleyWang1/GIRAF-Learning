# Whiteboard checkmark dataset audit

Dataset: `data/whiteboard_checkmark/whiteboard_checkmark_boom_40in.zarr`.
Snapshot: 211 committed episodes (zero-based IDs 0–210), 79,864 steps. Episode ends were unchanged throughout the main scan. Opened dataset read-only.

## Findings

- 462 alignment-invalid steps (0.5785%); 79,402 valid steps. 179 episodes have at least one invalid step; 32 have none. Longest invalid run is two steps (about 67 ms at 30 Hz).
- 145 steps have control age outside 0–50 ms; 331 have motor age outside 0–50 ms, with 14 steps in both groups. All recorded invalid flags match these timing failures. No motor dispatch rejection flags.
- Five clutch 1→0 transitions, in episodes 67, 128, 135, 150, and 174. Episode 0 starts clutch-off for 33 steps. Total clutch-off: 437 steps across six episodes. Episode 174 is the only interior release followed by re-engagement; the other releases extend to the final recorded step.
- Tracking is off despite clutch-on for 223 steps in episode 150 (5.598–13.029 s) and 92 steps in episode 174 (4.265–7.331 s). These flags establish a tracking interruption; they do not identify whether pose freshness or motor readiness caused it. Episode 174 resumes tracking at step 316 (10.530 s); episode 150 does not resume before the episode ends.
- 752 total tracking-off steps, including clutch-off steps. Alignment validity does not require clutch-on or tracking-on.
- All aligned IMU samples are valid. No reported producer or saver IMU drops. Accelerometer metadata records 72,018 sequence gaps, while gyroscope and rotation vector record none. Accelerometer report rates remain approximately 99.97–100.10 Hz; intra-episode timestamp spacing is 7.364–16.639 ms. These sequence gaps are a separate acquisition observation, not aligned IMU validity failures or confirmed host drops.
- No camera sequence gaps within episodes, no nonincreasing frame timestamps, and no NaN/Inf in action or state arrays.
- Every episode has a camera.mp4 and metadata file. Stored valid/invalid/IMU-valid counts match recomputed counts and episode JSON. All data arrays have 79,864 rows. No partial entries were present. Video files were checked for existence, not fully decoded; task success was not assessed visually.
- Total first-to-last-frame duration is 2,654.19 s (44.24 min); episode lengths range from 164 to 630 steps, with median 358 steps (~11.9 s).

## Conventions and files

Episode IDs and step indices are zero-based. Invalid means `alignment_valid == 0`; IMU validity is separate. Clutch drop means a recorded 1→0 transition within an episode; initial clutch-off is counted separately. Times are relative to the first saved camera frame. Off-window end times use the next saved frame, or one nominal 30 Hz frame interval beyond the last sample for terminal windows; these are sampled estimates of transitions.

- `episodes.csv`: all 211 episodes, with invalid causes, clutch and tracking counts, timing maxima, IMU diagnostics, and video existence.
- `events.csv`: individual invalid, clutch-off, tracking-off-with-clutch-on, and IMU-invalid runs, with inclusive step ranges and time windows.
- `summary.json`: aggregate results and anomalous event details.
